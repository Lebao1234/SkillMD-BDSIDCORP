Careers

Build a career in Dong Anh

We hire skilled workers and people who want to learn the trade. Stable shifts, on-the-job training and a path to team leader.

HR-01Stainless steel and steel welder4 positions · Full time

Duties

  • TIG welding of stainless steel, MIG welding of steel frames to drawing
  • Grinding and brushing welds, finishing products
  • Installing railings and stairs on site when needed

Requirements

  • At least 1 year of stainless steel welding experience
  • Able to read simple drawings
  • Helpers without experience will be trained
HR-02Laser cutting and press brake operator2 positions · Full time

Duties

  • Operate the fiber laser cutter and CNC press brake
  • Read CAD files, nest parts to save material

Requirements

  • Experience on a press brake or laser preferred
  • Full personal protective equipment provided
HR-03Drafting and estimating technician1 position · Full time

Duties

  • Measure samples and redraw production drawings
  • Material take-off and quotations
  • Work with customers and the production team

Requirements

  • Vocational diploma or higher in mechanical engineering
  • Proficient in AutoCAD and 3D software (SolidWorks, Inventor)
