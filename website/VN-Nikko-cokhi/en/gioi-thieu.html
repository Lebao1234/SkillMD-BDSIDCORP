About

A stainless steel and steel fabrication workshop in Dong Anh.

VN - NIKKO Mechanical Co., Ltd. makes products to order and from samples for household, civil works, furniture, 5S items, industrial park items and custom parts.

Letter of introduction

Dear customers and partners,

First of all, VN - NIKKO Mechanical Co., Ltd. would like to send our customers and partners our respectful greetings and sincere thanks for your interest in our products and services.

VN - NIKKO was founded to give households, contractors and factories stainless steel and steel products that match the sample, last, and arrive on time. We have invested in laser cutting machines, CNC press brakes, welding machines and a finishing line in our own workshop, so we control quality at every step, whether the order is one piece or thousands.

We look forward to a long-term relationship with our customers and partners, built on cooperation, transparency and mutual growth.

Sincerely,Board of Directors, VN - NIKKO Mechanical Co., Ltd.

Quality commitment

Checks at every step.

Four checkpoints apply to every order, from single parts to batch production.

  1. The stainless steel grade quoted

    The grade is stated on the quote, and customers can test it with a reagent on delivery.

  2. Sample approval before batch production

    The customer checks the sample's dimensions and finish before we run the full quantity.

  3. Clean welds, even surfaces

    Stainless steel welds are ground and pickled; surfaces are evenly brushed or mirror polished.

  4. One point of responsibility

    Cutting, bending, welding and painting in one workshop, with no passing of responsibility between subcontractors.
