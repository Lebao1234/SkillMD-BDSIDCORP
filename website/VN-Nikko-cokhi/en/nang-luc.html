Capabilities

Good machines, skilled hands.

Cutting, bending, welding, machining and surface finishing are all done at the Vong La workshop, so products do not move back and forth between contractors.

Cutting
Fiber laser, hydraulic shearing, pipe cutting to length
Bending
CNC bending up to 3,200 mm, pipe bending, sheet rolling
Welding
TIG for stainless steel, MIG/CO2 for steel frames, handheld laser welding
Machining
Turning, milling and drilling parts to drawing and sample
Finish
Brushed and mirror finishes for stainless steel; powder coating, galvanizing

Process

Seven steps with set timelines.

Applies to orders made to drawing and orders made from samples.

01/07
  1. 01

    Day 0

    Request received

    We receive drawings, samples, photos or hand sketches. An engineer confirms material, quantity and place of use.

  2. 02

    If needed

    Site survey and measurement

    On-site measurement at the project or factory for railings, stairs, warehouse racks and 5S items.

  3. 03

    Within 24 hours

    Quote and drawing

    A quote with a drawing or fabrication plan, material and finish for the customer to approve.

  4. 04

    2 - 5 days

    First sample

    For batch orders, we make one sample for the customer to check dimensions and finish.

  5. 05

    Depends on quantity

    Production

    Laser cutting, bending, welding, turning and milling to the approved drawing.

  6. 06

    1 - 3 days

    Surface finishing

    Grinding, brushing and polishing for stainless steel; powder coating or galvanizing for steel.

  7. 07

    1 day

    Inspection, delivery, installation

    Checks on dimensions, welds and surface. Packing, delivery to site and installation on request.

Quality

What we check and when.

  1. Material

    Correct stainless steel grade and thickness

    Grade checked with a reagent or analyzer; sheet and pipe thickness measured before cutting.

  2. First sample

    Sample approval before batch production

    The customer checks the sample's dimensions, finish and fit before we run the full quantity.

  3. After welding

    Welds and squareness

    Welds checked for full penetration and no porosity; frame squareness and flatness measured before finishing.

  4. Finish

    Surface and coating

    Even brushing on stainless steel with no weld burn marks; powder coating at full thickness and the right colour code.

  5. Before delivery

    Dimensions and packing

    Installation dimensions measured, protective film on stainless steel, crated to prevent scratches in transit.
