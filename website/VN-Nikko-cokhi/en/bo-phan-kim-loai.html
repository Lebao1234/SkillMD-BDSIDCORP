FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I order without a drawing?

Yes. Send photos of the sample and a few key dimensions, or bring the sample to the workshop. A technician measures it, redraws the drawing and sends it for your approval before production.

Do you take small orders?

Yes. The workshop accepts orders from 1 piece. For batch orders, we make one sample for your approval before running the full quantity.
